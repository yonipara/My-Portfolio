About Me

About Me

I’m a developer with a strong foundation in software engineering, having studied core computer science principles for one year before diving deeper into AI‑powered full‑stack web development for six months. My path into tech wasn’t accidental — I’ve always been drawn to problem‑solving, logical reasoning, and the kind of critical thinking that makes computer science so rewarding.

Over time, I discovered how much I enjoy building things that work, breaking down complex challenges, and turning ideas into real applications. Whether it’s designing a clean interface or structuring a backend system, I approach every project with curiosity and a desire to understand how each piece fits together.

I’m committed to growing as a developer, learning new technologies, and sharpening my ability to think clearly and solve problems effectively. My goal is to create meaningful digital experiences while continuously improving my craft and expanding my technical capabilities.
